# Hotec website mockup

Design mockup for Hotec, a supplier of kitchen appliances and hospitality equipment in the Maldives.

Live preview: https://muaaadh.github.io/hotec-mockup/

## Pages

- `index.html`: home
- `products.html`: catalogue with filters and product details
- `order.html`: order as a customer or as a business, with quotation requests
- `account.html`: customer and business accounts, order history

## Notes

- Static site, no build step. Open `index.html` or serve the folder.
- Product data lives in `assets/js/data.js`.
- This is a mockup. Prices, ratings and reviews are sample values, and orders and accounts are kept in the browser only.
