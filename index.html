How do I order?
Add products to your order on this site and send it to us. You can also message us on Viber at +960 726 4767, send an Instagram message, or visit us in-store.
What is the difference between a customer account and a business account?
A customer account is for buying for your home. A business account is for restaurants, guest houses, resorts and companies: you can ask for a quotation before you order, and your invoice is made out in your company name.
Can I see a product before I buy it?
Yes. Visit us in-store and our team will show you the product and answer your questions.
Do you deliver to other islands?
Tell us your island when you order. We will confirm the delivery options and the cost with you before you pay.
How do I pay?
We confirm your order first, then send you the payment details. Business accounts receive a quotation and an invoice.
Do products come with a warranty?
Warranty depends on the brand and the product. We will confirm the cover for your item before you pay.
Can you supply a full kitchen or a large order?
Yes. Open a business account, send us your list and we will prepare a quotation for everything on it.
